Analyzing high blood pressure
=============================

We use data from the Second National Health

~~~~
<<dd_do:quietly>>
webuse nhanes2, clear
<</dd_do>>
~~~~

## Logistic regression results

We fit a logistic regression model of high

~~~~
<<dd_do>>
logistic highbp weight agegrp##sex, nopvalues vsquish
<</dd_do>>
~~~~

##Interaction plot

Below we have a figura about interaction between Age and Sex

~~~~
<<dd_do:quietly>>
margins agegrp##sex
marginsplot, title (Age Group and Sex Interaction) ///
		ytitle(Expected Probability of High Blood Pressure)
<</dd_do>>
~~~~

<<dd_graph: saving("interaction.png") replace height(400)>>
